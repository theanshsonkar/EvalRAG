# Admissions
Applications for the B.Tech programme open on 1 March and close on 30 April every year.
Applicants must have at least 60 percent marks in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics in Class 12.
The application fee is 1500 rupees and it is non-refundable.
Shortlisted candidates are called for a personal interview in the second week of May.
Admission results are published on the university portal by 31 May.
