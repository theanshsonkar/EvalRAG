# Hostel
The university has separate hostels for boys and girls with a total capacity of 3000 students.
Hostel rooms are allotted on a first come first served basis after fee payment.
The hostel fee is 90,000 rupees per year and includes meals in the mess.
Hostel gates close at 10 PM and students must sign the register for late entry.
Guests are allowed in the visitor lounge between 4 PM and 7 PM.
