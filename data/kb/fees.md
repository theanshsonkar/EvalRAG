# Fees and Scholarships
The annual tuition fee for the B.Tech programme is 2,40,000 rupees.
Fees can be paid in two instalments, one in July and one in January.
A late fee of 500 rupees per week is charged after the due date.
Merit scholarships cover 50 percent of tuition for students with a CGPA of 9.0 or above.
Scholarship renewal requires the student to maintain a CGPA of 8.5 every year.
