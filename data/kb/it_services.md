# IT Services
Every student receives a university email account and a campus Wi-Fi login on joining.
Wi-Fi passwords must be changed every ninety days.
Lost ID cards can be reissued at the IT helpdesk for a fee of 300 rupees.
The IT helpdesk is located in Block C, room 104, and is open from 9 AM to 6 PM.
Licensed software such as MATLAB is available through the university software portal.
