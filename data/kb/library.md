# Library
The central library is open from 8 AM to 11 PM on all working days.
Students can borrow up to four books at a time for a period of fourteen days.
A fine of 5 rupees per day is charged for each overdue book.
The library provides free access to IEEE Xplore and the ACM Digital Library on campus.
Group study rooms can be booked online for up to two hours per day.
